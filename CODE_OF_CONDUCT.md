# Code of Conduct

We aim to provide a welcoming, respectful space for everyone. Be considerate, communicate constructively, respect differing experiences, and accept feedback in good faith. Harassment, discrimination, threats, and disclosure of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary. Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer through an available channel; include enough context to assess the concern without publishing sensitive details.
